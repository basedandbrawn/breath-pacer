Wind Bender Auras

Three worlds the app could live in, each running live: a mystical one, one where the wind is being bent, and a dark sky of clouds. Toggle each phone between its home screen and a set. Below them, three ways the four modes could be chosen, with what each mode is and which lifts belong to it.

Same app, same words, same two notes. What changes is the world the breath moves through. Each phone runs Drive, 3 · 1 · 2, on a loop; switch it to Home to see the wordmark in that world.

B · Pick your lift

People think in lifts, not shapes. Search or scroll a list grouped by shape; tapping a lift picks its shape for you and names the set. The run screen's meta line reads REAR DELT FLY · SQUEEZE, so the next time you are on that machine you never have to remember which mode it was.

C · Shape first, with a live rep

Four shapes in one row. The chosen one plays a rep continuously above its lifts, the word and breath line changing as it would on the run screen, so you see exactly what you are about to do before you tap Start. The lift chips name the set.

Recommendation: B, with C's live rep at the top of it

Pick your lift is the flow that matches how the choice is actually made at the machine, and it removes the mode vocabulary from the decision entirely: you never choose Squeeze, you choose rear delt fly and Squeeze comes with it. Put C's playing rep above the list, following whichever lift is highlighted, and the what-line under it. A's chip rows become the lift list's groups. Reps and sets stay where they are, below.

03 · Notes

What each aura would change

  • Mystic. Cormorant Garamond for the wordmark and the cue, italic for the line under it, so LOWER reads like a word on an old page. Ground violet-black rising to plum; incense smoke in lavender, drawn into a gold ring that fills like a mandala as the lungs do; the ring pulses on a hold. The tone would suit a bowl pair. Quietest of the three; best for Breathe, maybe too soft for a gym.
  • Bender. The move. Crescent slashes and speed lines converge in a spiral on the breath in and burst outward on the out, bone white with a cold cyan core, on ink; a vermilion seal for the rep count. A burst ring marks every turn of the breath, which is also what the eye needs mid-set. Loudest, fastest, most gym.
  • Dark Sky. Not smoke: cloud masses, lit from behind by a moon that brightens as the lungs fill. Abstract, high contrast, edges lit and cores dark, churning fast; the clouds surge toward the moon on the in and roll away on the out, and stand still, lit, on a hold. The luminance change of the whole screen is the largest of the three, which is what peripheral vision reads.
  • Modes. Whichever layout: every mode carries a what in plain words and its list of lifts, and the run screen names the lift. That is the whole UX change, and it costs nothing at the bar.
